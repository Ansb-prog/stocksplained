# Stocksplained: project brief

## Overview

Stocksplained was pitched at the Applied Thinking Challenge, hosted at Ajman University in collaboration with the Department of Finance. It translates stock market data into plain language for UAE residents aged 40–60. It explains metrics; it never replaces them or gives advice.

**One-liner:** Stocksplained turns confusing stock market numbers into plain language, so adults aged 40–60 can understand their investments with confidence.

**Taglines:** Markets, minus the jargon. · Your stocks, in plain words. · السوق بكلام بسيط (the market in simple words)

## Problem statement

Many UAE residents aged 40–60, both Emiratis and long-term expats, are at the peak of their earning years. They have clear goals: a comfortable retirement, their children's education, owning property, and leaving something for their families. Yet many keep their savings in current accounts or cash because the stock market feels confusing, risky and built for experts.

Uninvested money slowly loses value. At 3% annual inflation, AED 100,000 left in cash buys only about AED 74,000 worth of goods after 10 years.

Those who want to invest face real barriers. Platforms are full of jargon like P/E ratio, dividend yield and volatility. Learning resources are too technical or made for younger users. Without trusted guidance, many rely on tips from friends, WhatsApp groups or social media, which exposes them to poor decisions and scams.

Stocksplained addresses this in three steps:

- **Awareness:** show users, with their own numbers, how much value idle savings lose over time.
- **Understanding:** explain stock market metrics in plain Arabic and English using familiar local comparisons.
- **Action:** teach beginner-friendly strategies (long-term investing, diversification, monthly investing, dividend investing) with step-by-step guides.

## Target user

| Attribute | Profile |
| --- | --- |
| Age | 40–60 |
| Location | UAE: Emiratis and long-term expat residents |
| Profile | Working professionals, business owners, pre-retirees with savings |
| Investing knowledge | Beginner to low; may hold a broker account without fully understanding it |
| Digital habits | Comfortable with smartphones and WhatsApp; finds finance apps confusing |
| Goals | Retirement, children's education, property, family legacy |
| Needs | Clear explanations, Arabic and English, confidence before acting, scam protection |
| Pain points | Jargon, fear of losing money, not knowing who to trust, feeling too old to start |

## Core features

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Tap-to-explain | Tap any metric on a stock page for a plain explanation, a local analogy and the normal range for that sector |
| Three depth levels | Simple, intermediate and professional, so users learn at their own pace |
| Inflation calculator | Shows users, with their own numbers, what idle cash loses over time |
| Strategy guides | Step-by-step guides to long-term investing, diversification, monthly investing and dividend investing |
| WhatsApp assistant | Send a broker app screenshot and get it explained, with no new app to download |
| Scam check | Paste a suspicious investment tip and see the warning signs |
| Sharia-compliance explainer | Shows whether a company is screened as compliant and why |
| Accessible design | Arabic and English, large text, voice read-aloud, family mode for grown children to help parents |

**Local analogies**

- P/E ratio: how many years of rent it takes to pay back the villa you bought.
- Dividend yield: rent income from an apartment you own.
- Volatility: Sheikh Zayed Road traffic; same destination, unpredictable ride.

## Future goals

- **Demo trading:** a practice portfolio with virtual money, so users can apply strategies and build confidence with no risk.
- **Copy trading:** beginners follow and learn from experienced, verified investors' portfolios, with every move explained in plain language. Copy trading is licensed in the UAE, so this would run through a partnership with a licensed broker.

## Judge Q&A

| Likely question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Isn't ELI5 condescending to 40–60 year olds? | We keep the method, not the label: "explain it simply" with adult, local analogies |
| Why not just ask ChatGPT? | We explain the specific stock on the user's screen, in context, with sector ranges and local analogies |
| Is jargon really the barrier? | Partly; fear, trust and scams matter too, so we add the scam check and inflation calculator |
| Can simplifying mislead people? | Three depth levels and sector context stop half-truths like "low P/E = cheap" |
| Isn't teaching strategies financial advice? | Strategies are general education; we never say "buy X". SCA and DFSA rules are respected |
| Who pays? | A simple subscription, plus B2B licensing to banks, brokers or ADX/DFM to grow their investor base |

## Limitations

- Education only; no predictions or buy/sell recommendations.
- Start narrow, such as ADX/DFM-listed stocks.
- Live market data costs money to license.
- Needs Arabic and English, possibly Hindi and Urdu later.
- AI explanations need accuracy checks.
- Impact must be measured, such as understanding scores before and after use.
- Copy trading requires a licensed broker partner.
